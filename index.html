Work out what's actually affecting how you feel

If something has been bothering you for months and nobody can tell you why, the missing piece is usually evidence. This is a private notebook that collects it, tests it properly, and turns it into one page you can hand to a doctor.

Start

What it does

  • Tracks the symptoms you actually have. You name them — not a fixed list of somebody else's idea of wellness.
  • Looks for what moves with them. Across your own log, at a one- and two-day lag, with the statistics done properly rather than by eye.
  • Runs real experiments. Pick one thing you suspect, vary it deliberately for a few weeks, and get an honest answer instead of a hunch.
  • Prints something a doctor can use. Dates, patterns, and what you have already ruled out — on one page, in ten minutes of appointment time.

What it doesn't do

It can't diagnose you, treat you, or cure anything, and it will never tell you what condition you have or what to take for it. It has no doctor in it. What it can do is stop you guessing — and make the guessing that's left somebody qualified's job, with better information in front of them.

It also won't invent patterns to keep you interested. On data with no real signal it says so and shows you nothing, which is the whole reason to trust it when it does show you something.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. Everything stays in your browser on this device — no account, no server, no analytics, nothing uploaded. That also means nobody is backing it up for you, so the export button matters.

VitalArc is a personal notebook, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and nothing in it is medical advice. Patterns it finds show what moves together in your log; they cannot prove one thing caused another. If something about your health worries you, or if a symptom is severe, new, or getting worse, please talk to a doctor rather than to this app.
