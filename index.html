How it works

Three things, done properly.

1 Log in forty seconds

Twenty habit fields across sleep, movement, nutrition, recovery and substances. Sliders with sensible defaults, so a normal day is a few taps.

2 Get a score you can argue with

Six pillars, each built from published dose-response curves rather than made-up point values. Eleven hours of sleep scores worse than eight, because that is what the evidence says. Every point is traceable.

3 Learn what's actually driving it

We test every habit against every outcome at several time lags, then apply a permutation test and a false-discovery-rate correction. What's left is worth acting on.

The part nobody else does

Finding real signal in one person's noisy daily log is genuinely hard, and getting it wrong is worse than saying nothing. Here is what we measured while building it.

VitalArc is a wellness and habit-tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. The Healthspan Age figure is an illustrative estimate derived from self-reported habits using published population-level relationships — it is not a clinical measurement of biological age, and it is shown with a confidence level reflecting how much of your data supports it. Correlations found in your log show what moves together; they cannot prove causation. Always consult a qualified clinician about your health.

Your data is stored locally in your browser. We do not upload it, and we do not run analytics on it.
