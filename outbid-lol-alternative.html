How pricing works

  • $5 claims a new spot. Bids stack — your total is your rank.
  • Taking any rank costs that rank's total plus $1; if it's your own spot, you only pay the difference.
  • The Today board resets at midnight UTC. Today's crown usually costs a few hundred dollars, not fourteen thousand.
  • No refunds. Obviously.

Why the daily reset matters

outbid.lol's own founder acknowledged the frozen-board problem when he launched a daily board days after launch: "You don't have to beat a $15k all-time #1 to get seen... everyone starts even again tomorrow." On OUTRANKED the daily war is the main event, and the all-time board is the hall of fame — so there's a winnable fight every single day, at every budget.
